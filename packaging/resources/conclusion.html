Open Mono Mic from Applications

  1. Choose your audio interface and microphone input.
  2. Allow Mono Mic Service to use the microphone when macOS asks.
  3. Select Mono Mic as the input in your voice app.

You can close settings while the service continues running. “Run service at startup” controls whether the service starts at login; settings stay closed.

To uninstall, open /Library/Application Support/MonoMic/Uninstall Mono Mic.command. Your saved audio settings are kept.
