Mono Mic

Route one audio-interface input to a mono virtual microphone for Discord and other voice apps.

This installs Mono Mic in Applications and its virtual audio driver. Administrator authorization is required. Audio playback and recording will briefly stop while macOS reloads its audio devices.

Your existing audio selections are preserved. Other BlackHole devices are kept.

After installation, open Mono Mic to select your interface and input and allow microphone access.
